## Are Cook Out Milkshakes Thick?

Cook Out milkshakes have a thick and creamy consistency. A straw may feel difficult to use when the shake is first served.

Let the shake sit for a short time if you prefer a softer texture. You can also ask for a spoon.

## Cook Out Milkshake Calories and Ingredients

Milkshakes can contain significant amounts of calories and sugar. Candy, cookies, and other mix-ins can increase the amount further.

### Tips for a Smaller Treat

* Choose a smaller size when available.
* Choose a simpler flavor without candy pieces.
* Share a shake if you want a smaller portion.

### Food Allergy Information

Anyone with a food allergy should ask about ingredients before ordering. Some flavors may contain dairy, peanuts, tree nuts, wheat, or other allergens.

Ask the restaurant about its current allergen information if you have a serious food allergy.
